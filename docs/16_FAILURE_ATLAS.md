## Where fmincon still wins (development + validation + held-out, track A)

* fmincon-ip attains HS2 and HS16's published basins where mincon lands in
  another strict local minimum (cluster 6), and BADSTART_DISC where mincon
  stops early (cluster 16, defect); it reaches the RANKLOSS_JAC-type degenerate
  points with fewer iterations by stopping earlier.
* fmincon-sqp and SLSQP use 0.6–0.75× the evaluations of any interior-point
  code on the small dense problems that dominate this corpus (cluster: no
  SQP in mincon yet; `docs/03_SPEC_SQP.md`).
* On HS38, HS56, HS63 and ELLIPSOID2_20 fmincon-ip needs fewer evaluations than C4 (clusters 8, 13).
* At n ≥ 200 with finite differences fmincon-ip fails at its evaluation cap while mincon spends a large budget (cluster 15). The C7 rule attained QUADSPHERE2_300, QUADSPHERE_1000 and ELLIPSOID_500 and cut MAXENT_200 to 0.157× on the development corpus, but cost 1.24× on the sealed round-4 set and is off by default (`s6v4-final4`). The non-separable CHAINROSEN_BOX_200 and ELLIPSOID2_200 remain the most expensive (structured/exact Hessian, cluster 4).
* Round 4 (`s6v4-final4`): SciPy SLSQP attains all 11 sealed problems on both tracks and needs 0.64× fmincon-ip's evaluations where mincon needs 0.85×; on dense convex QPs (covqp, obstacle) an active-set SQP with an exact QP subproblem is the right algorithm and mincon's SQP member takes 3–5× SLSQP's iterations. Wall time on those model-bound problems is 1.4–1.9× fmincon's, 97 % of it in the Python model callbacks.

## Where mincon wins

* Robustness at defaults: dev 77/81 vs 73/81 (5 wins / 1 loss), validation
  23/24 vs 21/24 (3 wins / 1 loss), with fmincon's losses concentrated in
  its evaluation cap (cluster 9) and degenerate HS13.
* Wall time on the same host, single thread: geo-mean ratio 0.008 on the
  development split (median 0.8 ms vs 120 ms); still faster at n = 600
  (LQTRAJ_200, 1.6 s vs 2.3 s).
